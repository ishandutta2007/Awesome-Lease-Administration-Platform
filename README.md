# Awesome-Lease-Administration-Platform

## Top Lease Administration Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Lease Accounting (ASC 842 / IFRS 16), Portfolio Management, Critical Dates, Abstracting & Real Estate / Equipment Leases*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Lease Administration**. These systems help organizations manage lease portfolios (real estate, equipment, fleet), maintain abstracts, track critical dates, calculate accounting entries under ASC 842 / IFRS 16 / GASB, and support compliance and operational reporting.



**Examples** include Visual Lease, LeaseAccelerator, IBM TRIRIGA, Planon, Archibus, Leasecake, Nakisa Lease Administration, ProLease, Lucernex, and Lease Harbor (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise lease administration and accounting platforms are almost entirely commercial due to complex accounting standards and portfolio scale. Practical open options exist mainly in **property management systems with lease modules** (e.g., ERPNext PropMS, MicroRealEstate, OpenProperty) and simple lease-accounting tools. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Visual Lease](https://www.visuallease.com/)**  

  Leading lease optimization and administration platform for centralizing real estate and equipment leases, supporting ASC 842 / IFRS 16 compliance, and improving portfolio performance.



- **[LeaseAccelerator](https://www.leaseaccelerator.com/)**  

  Enterprise lease lifecycle automation solution providing asset-level data for finance, real estate, treasury, and procurement stakeholders.



- **[IBM TRIRIGA](https://www.ibm.com/products/tririga)**  

  Intelligent real estate and facilities management (IWMS) platform with strong lease administration, space, and asset capabilities.



- **[Planon](https://planonsoftware.com/)**  

  Integrated workplace management system (IWMS) that includes lease and real estate portfolio administration features.



- **[Archibus](https://archibus.com/)**  

  Facilities and real estate management platform supporting lease administration within broader IWMS workflows.



- **[Leasecake](https://leasecake.com/)**  

  Lease management platform aimed at multi-unit operators and franchisees for tracking locations, critical dates, and lease obligations.



- **[Nakisa Lease Administration](https://www.nakisa.com/)**  

  Cloud-native lease accounting and administration software for complex global portfolios (real estate, equipment, fleet) with ERP integration.



- **[ProLease](https://www.prolease.com/)**  

  Lease administration and accounting solution used by corporate real estate and finance teams.



- **[Lucernex (Accruent)](https://www.accruent.com/)**  

  Real estate lifecycle and lease management capabilities within the Accruent portfolio.



- **[Lease Harbor](https://www.leaseharbor.com/)**  

  Lease administration and accounting platform focused on compliance and portfolio visibility.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[PropMS (ERPNext Property Management)](https://github.com/inayatkh/propms)**  

  Property management solution on ERPNext that includes lease management along with related operational modules (requires ERPNext).



- **[MicroRealEstate](https://github.com/microrealestate/microrealestate)**  

  Self-hosted property management app for landlords to manage leases, rents, and receipts with template-based lease generation.



- **[OpenProperty and similar open property systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source rental property management platforms supporting units, tenants, leases, rent collection, and maintenance.



- **[Simple lease accounting tools (e.g., LeaseReg-style)](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open applications for tracking lease contracts, schedules, and basic lessee accounting entries under local standards.



- **[ERPNext / Frappe real estate and asset modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open-source ERP components that can be configured for asset tracking, contracts, and basic lease-related accounting.



- **[General open accounting and fixed-asset systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Open double-entry accounting platforms that can be extended to record right-of-use assets and lease liabilities.



- **[Critical-date and contract tracking open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open systems for storing contract abstracts, renewal dates, and reminder workflows.



- **[Document and abstract open repositories](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools for storing and versioning lease documents and extracted key terms.



- **[Reporting and schedule open generators](https://github.com/)**  

  Scripts and notebooks that produce amortization schedules and basic lease accounting reports from structured inputs.



- **[Integration and ETL open helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Connectors for moving lease data between spreadsheets, ERPs, and custom databases.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **ERPNext + PropMS** or similar open property stacks for operational lease tracking in smaller portfolios.

- Adopting self-hosted landlord/property tools (MicroRealEstate, OpenProperty) when the primary need is rent and lease lifecycle management rather than full ASC 842/IFRS 16 accounting.

- Building simple lease schedules and journals on top of open accounting systems for low-volume portfolios.

- Accepting that enterprise-scale lease accounting compliance, multi-currency/multi-GAAP support, deep ERP integration, and portfolio analytics still require commercial platforms (Visual Lease, LeaseAccelerator, Nakisa, IBM TRIRIGA, Planon, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on operational lease data and transparency rather than replacing full lease administration suites.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Store lease abstracts and documents in an open property or contract system → generate payment and amortization schedules → post accounting entries to an open or commercial ERP → track critical dates with reminders. Suitable for small portfolios or internal tooling. Most mid-size and large organizations continue to rely on commercial lease administration platforms for compliance and audit readiness.
